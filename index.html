My AI Assistant Igor

A personal AI assistant that helps its owner manage their own Google account from a private chat.

What the app does

My AI Assistant Igor is a private, single-user application. The owner sends it a text or voice message, and the assistant carries out the request in the owner's own Google account:

  • Google Calendar — shows the schedule, creates, moves and cancels meetings.
  • Gmail — finds and reads messages, prepares and sends emails on request.
  • Google Drive, Docs and Sheets — finds, reads, creates and edits the owner's files.
  • Google Apps Script — reads and runs the owner's own scripts.

Who can use it

The app is not a public service. It is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account, and it runs on the owner's private server.

Why it asks for Google access

The assistant can only do what is listed above after the owner signs in with Google and grants access. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
