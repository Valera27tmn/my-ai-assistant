Privacy Policy

My AI Assistant Igor · Last updated: October 9, 2026

Summary

My AI Assistant Igor is a private, single-user application. It is used only by its owner with the owner's own Google account. It has no other users and does not collect data from anyone else.

What Google data the app accesses

After the owner signs in with Google and grants access, the app can access the owner's Google Calendar, Gmail, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Apps Script data. It accesses this data only to carry out a request the owner has made.

How the data is used

  • Data is used only to fulfil the owner's requests, such as showing the schedule, creating a meeting, finding an email or editing a document.
  • To understand a request and prepare an answer, the relevant content is processed by the AI model provider the owner has connected to the assistant.
  • Data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else.

Where the data is stored

The Google access tokens and the assistant's conversation history are stored on the owner's private server. Access to that server is restricted to the owner.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that the app can no longer reach the Google account. Stored tokens and history can be deleted from the server by the owner.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent by opening an issue in the GitHub repository that hosts this site.
